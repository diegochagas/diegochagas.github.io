# Dell P2719H Monitor Setup: Stand, Cables and Pivot, Step by Step

<PostDate date={date} />

The Dell P2719H quick setup guide is one folded sheet with seven drawings.
Mine came with a 27-inch P2719HE, and the guide is the same. Here are the
drawings split into steps, one image each, with what they show.

## What comes in the box

![Contents of the Dell P2719H box](/blog/dell-monitor-setup/box.jpg)

The monitor, the stand with its base, the documentation, and four cables: a
power cord and the signal cables (HDMI, DisplayPort and a USB cable for the
monitor's USB ports).

## Step 1: join the stand and the base

![Step 1: stand and base](/blog/dell-monitor-setup/step-1.jpg)

The drawing has three small moves. First lift the tab on the stand's base
plate (1), then slide the stand onto the base (2). Finally turn the screw
under the base to lock them together (3).

## Step 2: attach the stand to the monitor

![Step 2: stand clipped onto the monitor](/blog/dell-monitor-setup/step-2.jpg)

Lay the monitor face down on its own box, on the foam or on a soft cloth.
Put the hooks of the stand into the slots at the back of the monitor (1) and
press down until it clicks (2).

## Step 3: connect the signal cables

![Step 3: HDMI, DisplayPort and USB cables](/blog/dell-monitor-setup/step-3.jpg)

At the back of the monitor you have an HDMI port, a DisplayPort (DP) port, a
VGA port and a USB upstream port, plus two USB ports on the side. The drawing
shows the three cables going to the computer: HDMI to an HDMI output, DP to a
DisplayPort output, and USB to a USB port of the PC. You only need one video
cable. Use the USB cable only if you want to use the monitor's USB ports.
Route the cables through the hole in the stand.

## Step 4: power

![Step 4: power cord](/blog/dell-monitor-setup/step-4.jpg)

Plug the power cord into the connector at the back of the monitor and the
other end into the wall socket.

## Step 5: turn it on

![Step 5: power button](/blog/dell-monitor-setup/step-5.jpg)

The power button is on the bottom right corner of the front. Press it and the
monitor lights up.

## Tip: tilt and rotate without hitting the base

![Tip: how to tilt and rotate the monitor safely](/blog/dell-monitor-setup/tip-tilt-pivot.jpg)

The left drawing, with the X, shows what **not** to do: tilting the monitor
back while it sits low makes the corner hit the base. The right drawing,
with the check mark, shows the right way. First raise the monitor on the
stand (1), then tilt it back (2), and then turn it 90 degrees (3) to use it
in portrait.

## Drivers and manual

The last drawing in the guide, with a disc and a book, only points to
**Dell.com/P2719H**, where the full manual and drivers are available.
