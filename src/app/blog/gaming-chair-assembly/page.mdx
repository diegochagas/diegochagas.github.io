# How to Assemble the Pichau Donek Gaming Chair, Step by Step

<PostDate date={date} />

The manual that comes with the Pichau Donek gaming chair is a single sheet
with the parts list and six drawings packed together, no text at all. I
scanned it and split it into one image per step, with a short explanation of
what each drawing shows, so you can follow along with the chair on the floor.
You only need the Allen key (M) that comes in the box.

## The parts

![Parts list of the Pichau Donek gaming chair](/blog/gaming-chair-assembly/parts.jpg)

Each letter on the sheet is used in the drawings below:

- **A**: backrest
- **B**: seat
- **C**: headrest pillow
- **D**: lumbar pillow
- **E**: tilt mechanism
- **F**: two side covers, (a) and (b)
- **G**: gas lift
- **H**: five-star base
- **I**: 5 casters
- **J**: 12 bolts
- **K**: 2 small screws
- **L**: plastic sleeve that covers the gas lift
- **M**: Allen key
- **N**: the two armrests

## Step 1: base, casters and gas lift

![Step 1: casters and gas lift on the base](/blog/gaming-chair-assembly/step-1.jpg)

Press the five casters (I) into the holes at the ends of the base (H) until
they are firmly seated. Then drop the gas lift (G) into the hole at the
center of the base, and slide the plastic sleeve (L) down over it. The round
detail on the right shows how a caster stem goes into its hole.

## Step 2: tilt mechanism under the seat

![Step 2: tilt mechanism attached to the seat](/blog/gaming-chair-assembly/step-2.jpg)

Turn the seat upside down. Place the tilt mechanism (E) on its underside, with
the lever pointing to the side, and fasten it with four J bolts using the
Allen key.

## Step 3: armrests

![Step 3: armrests attached to the seat](/blog/gaming-chair-assembly/step-3.jpg)

With the seat still upside down, attach each armrest (N) to its side of the
seat with the J bolts. The drawing shows six bolts in total for the two
armrests.

## Step 4: backrest and pillows

![Step 4: backrest attached to the seat](/blog/gaming-chair-assembly/step-4.jpg)

Connect the backrest (A) to the back of the seat (B) with the bolts the
drawing shows, tightened with the Allen key. The headrest pillow (C) and the
lumbar pillow (D) go on the backrest with their straps. Count your J bolts
before you start: the parts list says 12, and steps 2 to 4 together ask for
more than that on paper, so if one is left over or missing, check the drawing
again.

## Step 5: first side cover and sitting the seat on the base

![Step 5: side cover (a) and seat onto the gas lift](/blog/gaming-chair-assembly/step-5.jpg)

Fit side cover (a) on one side of the seat mechanism with one of the small
screws (K), using the Allen key. Then lift the whole seat and lower it onto
the gas lift. The arrow on the right shows the direction: straight down.
Press it in until it sits all the way.

## Step 6: second side cover

![Step 6: side cover (b) on the other side](/blog/gaming-chair-assembly/step-6.jpg)

Do the same on the other side with side cover (b) and the second small screw
(K). The chair is done: roll it a bit, adjust the lever, and check that
nothing wobbles.
