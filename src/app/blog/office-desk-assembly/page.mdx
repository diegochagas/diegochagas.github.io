# How to Assemble the Mesa Office 51015 Monitor Desk, Step by Step

<PostDate date={date} />

The Mesa Office (51015) is a small black desk with a shelf. Its manual is a
single page in Portuguese, and the drawings carry all the information. I
scanned it and cropped each step, so here is what each one shows. The
drawings don't show any screws or tools: the pieces slide into each other.

## The pieces

![Exploded view and parts table of the Mesa Office 51015](/blog/office-desk-assembly/parts.jpg)

The table on the page lists five numbered pieces:

- **1**: top, 1 piece
- **2**: base, 1 piece
- **3**: back panel, 1 piece
- **4**: side panels, 2 pieces
- **5**: shelf, 1 piece

## Step 1: back panel and sides

![Step 1: side panels fitted into the back panel](/blog/office-desk-assembly/step-1.jpg)

Lay the back panel (3) flat. Slide the two side panels (4) into its ends, as
the arrows show. The panels have slots along the edge that line up.

## Step 2: base

![Step 2: the frame goes onto the base](/blog/office-desk-assembly/step-2.jpg)

Lower the frame you just made onto the base (2). The round detail in the
corner shows how the edge of the panel sits inside the groove of the base, so
look at it if the pieces don't want to go in.

## Step 3: top

![Step 3: the top goes on](/blog/office-desk-assembly/step-3.jpg)

Put the top (1) on the frame, with the arrows pointing at the upper edges of
the side and back panels. Press it down until it sits flat on the three sides.

## Step 4: the shelf, in one of two positions

![Step 4: sliding the shelf in, upper (a) or lower (b) position](/blog/office-desk-assembly/step-4.jpg)

The shelf (5) slides in from the open front, between the side panels. The
manual shows two options: **a** on the upper rails, **b** on the lower ones.
Pick the height that fits your monitor or keyboard. The shelf has a hole near
one corner, which is for cables, so keep it toward the back.

## Step 5: finished

![Step 5: finished desk with the shelf in the upper (a) or lower (b) position](/blog/office-desk-assembly/step-5.jpg)

This is what it looks like when done, with the shelf on the upper position
(a) or the lower one (b). Move it to where it will live before putting
anything on it.
